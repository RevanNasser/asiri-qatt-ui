## Direction notes

Toasts appear bottom-left in Arabic and bottom-right in English; dir is passed to Toaster. Swipe to dismiss towards inline-end.

## Accessible

Toasts are announced through a live region; the type is conveyed by icon and text, not by the stripe colour alone.
