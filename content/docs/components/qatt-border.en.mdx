A decorative edge on one or more sides of your content: a band of motifs that can end in khatmah squares, the way bands are closed on the wall.

<ComponentPreview name="qatt-border-demo" />

## Installation

<InstallCommand item="qatt-border" />

## Usage

Choose the sides with side. The top strip stands upright and the bottom strip is turned over, so the triangles point into the content. Keep at least the strip thickness plus 8px between the strip and the content.

```tsx
import { QattBorder } from "@/components/ui/qatt-border"

<QattBorder side="block" caps>
  …
</QattBorder>
```

## Direction notes

Logical sides, end caps and the paint reveal all follow the page direction. The motif artwork itself never flips.

## Accessible

Pure decoration, hidden from assistive technology. The inner padding stops the strip from covering the focus outline of the elements inside.
