A grid of painted cards presenting your product's features, each with an icon in a coloured khatmah-like square, over a very faint shabakah background.

## Usage

Three columns suit six features; two suit four. Keep each title to two or three words and the body to one sentence. Giving a feature an href makes the whole card clickable through a single inner link.

```tsx
import { QattFeatureGrid } from "@/components/qatt-feature-grid"

<QattFeatureGrid title="…" features={[{ title: "…", body: "…" }]} />
```

## Direction notes

The grid fills from inline-start; only directional icons flip.

## Accessible

Feature titles are h3 under the section heading; icons are decorative. A clickable card has one link, named by the feature title.
